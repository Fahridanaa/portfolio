### The problem

The RW at Villa Bukit Tidar needed one system for its administration: family and citizen records, letters, archives, and the zakat fitrah distribution. The zakat part needed a decision support step, so the amil could rank families from the same data the app already held.

### What I built

Sawira has four roles. Warga see their family record and send letters to the RT. RT manage their residents and life events such as a move or a death. RW manage the RTs and process the letters. Amil run the zakat fitrah distribution.

![Sawira dashboard with population charts](public/assets/projects/sawira/dashboard.png)

The team listed me as the front-end developer. In practice I wrote 60 of the repo's 67 non-merge commits, including the SAW and SMART services. I built the Blade layouts and sidebar, the dashboard cards and Chart.js charts, the DataTables behind the citizen, family, and archive screens, and the SAW and SMART wizard: criteria and weights, alternative values, normalization, weighting, ranking, and a PDF export of the result.

![SAW ranking screen for zakat fitrah](public/assets/projects/sawira/amil-zakat.png)

The ranking uses five criteria: income, dependents, housing condition, debt, and monthly expenses. SAW and SMART produce two rankings from the same data, and the amil can compare them before deciding.

### The result

The result is a working administration app with a decision trail: the amil can compute SAW and SMART rankings from the family data and export a PDF, while the citizen and family screens keep a history instead of overwriting a record. The team also fixed it for HTTPS hosting. The repository is public.
